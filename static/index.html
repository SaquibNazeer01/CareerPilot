⚡ Method A: Instant Cookie Connect (Recommended) No Popups Needed

Already logged into LinkedIn in your browser? Paste your li_at cookie value to connect instantly:

📖 How to copy your li_at cookie in 10 seconds
  1. In your browser where LinkedIn is open, press F12 (or right-click ➔ Inspect).
  2. Click the Application tab (or Storage in Firefox).
  3. In the left panel, expand Cookies ➔ click https://www.linkedin.com.
  4. Find li_at in the list, double-click its Value, copy it, and paste here!
or Method B: Desktop Browser Window
Stored safely in your local .env file. Never sent to any external server.
💡 To see the popup Chrome window on your monitor, start the studio via start_dashboard.bat on your desktop.
